Chess Review

A chess analysis app that doesn't just call a move a blunder, it explains why in normal sentences, like a coach would. It uses Stockfish for the analysis, but the explanations come from my own rules and templates, not from an AI model. I built it for myself, as a ~1100 player who wanted to understand his mistakes.

Python · Stockfish · JavaScript

!

Chess Openings Trainer

Imports my games from Chess.com and Lichess, shows the openings I actually play as a move tree, and lets me drill my repertoire with spaced repetition. Everything is stored locally in SQLite.

FastAPI · SQLite · React

PokerMath (private)

A Chrome side panel that reads a poker table and shows the maths: what a call costs and how much equity you have. It never plays for you, it only shows the numbers.
